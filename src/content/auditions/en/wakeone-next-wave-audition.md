## Ongoing applications

WAKEONE accepts online audition applications at any time. There are no restrictions on age, gender, or nationality, but applicants must be able to travel abroad if selected for the next stage.

## What to submit

- A self-introduction in Korean (foreign applicants may also introduce themselves in their native language) and a front-facing headshot.
- A landscape audition video under two minutes and 30 seconds for each category. Singing, dancing, rapping, and acting are accepted, and multiple categories may be shown.
- Contact details other than email, such as an SNS ID, phone number, or messenger ID.

Email the completed application and materials to **wakeone_audition@wake-one.com**. The [official WAKEONE audition page](https://wake-one.com/en/audition/) provides the application form and current submission guidance.

Results are sent only to selected applicants. The official FAQ says applicants may reapply in the following month, or sooner when updating their materials.
