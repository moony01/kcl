## Who can apply

Anyone born in 2008 or later may apply, regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category: vocal, rap, dance, or other (including instrument, acting, and composition).

## Pre-registration deadline

Online pre-registration is open from August 3 through 11:59 PM KST on August 23, 2026. There is no separate on-site registration. Applicants who pass the first round receive the detailed audition time and instructions by email between August 24 and 30.

## In-person audition schedule

The official schedule lists the following locations:

- September 5: Incheon
- September 6: Boryeong
- September 12: Gunsan and Haenam
- September 13: Mokpo
- September 16: Pohang
- September 20: Geoje
- October 4: Sokcho
- October 17: Jeju (Wolpyeong)
- October 18: Seogwipo

Submit your pre-registration through the [official SOURCE MUSIC audition application](https://sourcemusic.hybeaudition.com/en). Check the official notice for the venue details and any schedule changes before applying.
