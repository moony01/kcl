## Year-round applications

The YG online ongoing audition, `WWW.YG-AUDITION`, operates 365 days a year. Applications can be submitted online without a fixed closing date.

## Eligibility

- Applicants may apply regardless of age.
- International applicants and people living overseas may apply.
- Every gender is eligible.

## Categories

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance

## Result notification

Only successful applicants are contacted individually, normally within two to four weeks after applying. Consult the official audition page and its Q&A for details such as application limits and reapplication timing.

Year-round audition policies can change, so review the latest official instructions immediately before submitting an application.
