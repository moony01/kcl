## Key dates (closing soon)

- **Online pre-application deadline:** Monday, September 7, 2026 at 00:00 Japan Standard Time (JST)
- **Audition date:** Sunday, September 20, 2026
- **Time and venue notification date:** Friday, September 11, 2026

Walk-in applications will not be accepted. Only applicants selected from the online pre-application will receive the exact audition time and venue at the email address entered in their application. Applicants should also check their spam or junk folder.

## Eligibility

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply, regardless of gender or nationality.

## Categories

Choose **one category** that best demonstrates your ability:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance
- Other, including visual or acting

## How to apply and important notes

Submit an online pre-application through the official YG Audition website.

- Each person may apply only once across the entire 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION schedule.
- The applicant's face must be clearly visible in every submitted photo and video.
- Exact timing and venue details are emailed only to applicants who pass the pre-application.

Schedules and application availability may change. Check the official page again immediately before applying.
