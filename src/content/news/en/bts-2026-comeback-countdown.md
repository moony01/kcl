Spring 2026. The moment that ARMYs worldwide have been counting down to is finally approaching. The **complete reunion of all seven BTS members**. As the members who enlisted sequentially starting in 2022 have all completed their military service, the biggest comeback in K-pop history is now imminent.

RM recently posted on Weverse: "Let's make 2026 the Year of BTS." This wasn't just a casual greeting. This single sentence carries a powerful determination to once again dominate the global music market, transcending the two-year hiatus.

## The Complete Return: All Seven Members Discharged

BTS members' military service began with Jin in December 2022 and proceeded sequentially.

**Discharge Timeline:**
- **Jin**: Enlisted December 2022 → Discharged June 2024 (First to return)
- **J-Hope**: Enlisted April 2023 → Discharged October 2024
- **RM, V**: Enlisted December 2023 → Discharged June 2025
- **Jimin, Jung Kook**: Enlisted December 2023 → Discharged June 2025
- **SUGA**: Started alternative service September 2023 → Completed June 2025

By the second half of 2025, all seven members had returned to civilian life. The approximately **two-and-a-half-year wait** was finally over. Since their discharge, members have been individually connecting with fans, heightening anticipation for the group's return.

## Spring Comeback & Project Outlook

According to industry insiders, BTS's complete group comeback is expected in **April-May 2026**. HYBE is reported to have already initiated the comeback project in the second half of 2025.

### Expected Comeback Strategy

**1. New Studio Album Release**

BTS's last studio album 'Proof' (2022) was an anthology album. This time, an **entirely new studio album** appears to be in preparation. Songs that members worked on individually during their military service are likely to be included.

**2. Global Simultaneous Release Strategy**

A strategy targeting the global market, similar to the 'Dynamite' and 'Butter' era, is expected. However, there are observations that this time they may return to a **Korean-language title track**. Members reportedly expressed several times during their service that they "want to return to our roots."

**3. Fan Participation Promotions**

HYBE is reportedly planning **ARMY participation comeback events** through Weverse. This is expected to be a new form of promotion where fans worldwide can directly participate in the comeback process.

## World Tour Announcement: Reclaiming the Global Stage

The highlight of BTS's comeback is undoubtedly the **world tour**.

The industry expects BTS to begin a **stadium world tour** in the second half of 2026. BTS's last major tour, 'LOVE YOURSELF: SPEAK YOURSELF' (2019), sold out world-renowned stadiums including Wembley, Rose Bowl, and Stade de France, generating approximately **$116.8 million (about 150 billion KRW)** in revenue.

### Expected Tour Scale

- **North America**: 10-15 cities, 30+ stadium shows
- **Europe**: 8-10 cities, major arenas and stadiums
- **Asia**: Korea, Japan, major Southeast Asian cities
- **South America**: Brazil, Argentina, Mexico
- **Total Expected Attendance**: Over 2 million

Experts predict this tour will be the largest in BTS history. Some analysts suggest the fandom has actually **grown during the two-year hiatus**. Members' solo activities attracted new fans, while existing fans' loyalty only strengthened.
