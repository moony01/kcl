On the afternoon of March 10, 2026, BELIFT LAB posted a statement.

It was brief. Its impact was not.

"Through in-depth discussions with each of the members about the future they envision and the direction of the team, it became clear that Heeseung has his own distinct musical vision, and we have decided to respect it."

Heeseung — 24, the main vocal and center of ENHYPEN, the most visible face of the group since its debut — was leaving. ENHYPEN would continue as a six-member group.

The ENGENE fandom exploded.

## "The Direction Suggested by the Company" — Five Words That Changed Everything

Immediately after the official statement, Heeseung posted a handwritten letter on Weverse. It expressed gratitude to his members and fans, and shared hope for his solo future. But fans locked onto one sentence:

**"I decided to move forward in the direction suggested by the company."**

The moment that line went public, the conversation shifted entirely.

Was this Heeseung's choice — or BELIFT LAB's?

Across X (formerly Twitter), theqoo, and Weverse, fans unleashed a torrent of criticism at BELIFT LAB. The core question was simple: **Why couldn't Heeseung pursue solo activities while remaining in the group?**

BTS members juggled solo projects and group work for years. SHINee, EXO, and GOT7 members did the same. So why was Heeseung forced to choose?

BELIFT LAB offered no explanation.

## Six Years — What Heeseung Meant to ENHYPEN

ENHYPEN debuted in 2020 through Mnet's survival show *I-LAND*. From the beginning, Heeseung stood apart — an all-rounder capable of handling vocals, dance, and rap, who became the group's standard-bearer and visual center.

In six years, Heeseung and ENHYPEN built something real:

- Billboard 200 entry from their debut album
- Headlining North American arena tours
- Four consecutive sold-out shows for the fan meeting 'STAY in Our Little House' at Inspire Arena
- Successful fan meetings across Malaysia, Indonesia, and the Philippines in late 2025

Heeseung was at the center of all of it. Now he's leaving.

## The Fan Event Cancellations — A Red Flag in Plain Sight

Many fans are now reading the preceding weeks differently.

On the very day of the departure announcement — March 10 — Heeseung was absent from a scheduled fan video call event. The official reason given was "poor physical condition." But what caught fans' attention was that this was **the third time the event had been postponed**.

January. February. March. Three reschedulings, each citing "unavoidable circumstances" with no further explanation. And on the exact day the third cancellation was announced, the departure statement dropped.

Fans began to ask: **Was the departure already decided weeks ago? Was BELIFT LAB knowingly scheduling fan events while planning to announce his exit?**

## The One Direction Ghost — K-Pop Fandom's Worst Nightmare

The comparison international fans reached for first was **One Direction**.

In 2015, Zayn Malik left One Direction. The stated reason was burnout, pressure, and a desire for solo work. What followed was the de facto dissolution of one of the biggest pop groups in history.

ENHYPEN's fandom is feeling that same dread right now.

Gulf News captured the sentiment precisely: fans called the news **"worse than disbanding."** A full disbandment, at least, is shared. One member leaving alone — with questions that may never be answered — is something different entirely.

## HYBE's Pattern — Is This Becoming a Habit?

The reason ENGENE and the broader K-pop community are reacting so intensely goes beyond this single announcement.

**Look at HYBE's recent history with its labels:**

- The NewJeans-ADOR conflict (Min Hee-jin removed as CEO, ongoing contract disputes)
- NI-KI (ENHYPEN) international fan community tensions
- KATSEYE's uneven global rollout and visibility concerns

Each incident is technically isolated. But read consecutively, they form a pattern: artist-label relationships at HYBE labels are fracturing with alarming frequency. Heeseung's departure adds another entry to that list.

After the Min Hee-jin scandal, fan trust in HYBE subsidiaries is at a historic low. That backdrop makes it almost impossible for any HYBE-adjacent announcement to be received charitably.

## What Heeseung Said — And What Remains

In his letter, Heeseung wrote:

"Thanks to the members with whom I shared countless emotions, and to ENGENE, who always filled every empty space with their support, I was able to take step after step toward a dream that once felt out of reach."

That dream now moves in a new direction. Heeseung will remain under BELIFT LAB and prepare a solo album. ENHYPEN will continue as six.

But the question fans are asking hasn't been answered.

**Why couldn't he do both?**

## The MEARROW Take

Member departures are not new in K-pop. But this situation has qualities that set it apart.

**The timing** is suspicious — announced simultaneously with a third consecutive fan event cancellation, months before their sixth anniversary.

**The language** is unusual — "direction suggested by the company" is not standard phrasing in a voluntary departure statement.

**The context** is charged — HYBE fandom trust is at rock bottom following the Min Hee-jin fallout.

When these three factors collide, ENGENE isn't just grieving a member's departure. They're questioning whether the departure was ever truly his choice.

BELIFT LAB has not addressed these concerns. The silence grows louder with every hour.

What Heeseung's solo chapter looks like, and whether ENHYPEN's six-member era finds its footing — MEARROW will be watching.
