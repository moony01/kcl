## 5 Essential Strategies for Audition Success

With acceptance rates below 0.1% among tens of thousands of applicants, **strategic preparation** is essential.

| Strategy | Key Point | How to Practice |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **1. Dance First** | Dance skills evaluated on the spot | Master 2+ genres: hip-hop, contemporary, urban |
| **2. Match Company Style** | Song selection = your sense | Choose recent songs from agency artists that suit you |
| **3. Camera Practice** | Video auditions often come first | Practice while filming on smartphone, check expressions |
| **4. 30-Second Intro** | Genuine answer required | Practice natural answers to "Why idol?" and "Your strength?" |
| **5. Clean Social Media** | Agencies check all accounts | Delete controversial posts or switch to private |

## What Current Trainees Say About Audition Reality

"The most important thing at auditions is **not being nervous**."

A current trainee who passed the 2024 HYBE audition shares:

> "There were many applicants more skilled than me. But when they stood before the judges, everyone got so nervous they couldn't show half of what they had. I was nervous too, but I smiled through it. Later, the judge said 'you looked like you were having the most fun.'"

Another JYP trainee emphasizes **the importance of the waiting room**:

> "They're watching how you talk to other applicants in the waiting room, how you greet the staff. The audition starts before you even get on stage."
